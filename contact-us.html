Frequently Asked Questions

Working With SpiceLink

Do you provide test certificates with every batch?
Yes. All shipments from Spice Link - Trading FZE are accompanied by Certificates of Analysis (CoA) verifying moisture levels, purity, total ash, and microbiological safety standards.
Can I request sample packs before placing a commercial order?
Absolutely. We furnish prospective wholesale clients and culinary establishments with evaluation sample kits for our core powders (Turmeric, Red Chilli, Coriander, Cumin, Pepper) and masala blends.
What packaging sizes are available for export?
We package in multi-wall Kraft paper sacks with inner food-grade poly liners (10kg, 20kg, 25kg), corrugated cartons, or customized retail barrier pouches (100g, 250g, 500g, 1kg).
What is the shelf life of SpiceLink cold-ground spices?
Due to our nitrogen-flushed, moisture-barrier packaging and cold-grinding methods, our ground spices retain peak aroma for 18 to 24 months when stored in cool, dark environments.
Do you support private labeling and custom blends?
Yes. We offer private label programs with custom branding, tailored packaging, and proprietary masala blends developed to your restaurant chain or retail brand's exact recipe and quality specifications.
Which ports and markets do you ship to?
Our Global Distribution Hub manages export documentation, cold-storage warehousing, and consolidated shipping to GCC, Middle East, Africa, Europe, and Asia Pacific markets - including FCL and LCL dispatch.
